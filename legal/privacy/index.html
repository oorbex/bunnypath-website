Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2, 2026 · Effective May 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how long we keep it, and how we protect it.

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Account Information. When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and authentication credentials (or tokens from Apple Sign-In or Google Sign-In). We do not store credit card numbers.
  • Household Profile Fields. You may enter a first name or nickname and an age range for each child to help surface age-appropriate activities. These are optional parent-supplied labels used only inside your own account.
  • Activity Preferences. You may tell us about the kinds of activities and focus areas your household is interested in.
  • User-Submitted Content. You may optionally provide written feedback and ratings on activities. The app does not collect or upload photos.

Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage Data. Features used, activities viewed or saved, session duration, device type.
  • Device Information. Device model, OS version, app version, and a unique device identifier for push notifications.
  • Crash and Performance Data. Anonymized crash logs and metrics to improve stability.
  • Advertising Identifiers. If you have not limited ad tracking on your device, AdMob receives a device advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, GAID on Android), IP address, and ad-event context to serve and measure non-personalized ads. See Advertising.

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect last names, physical addresses, schools, photos, voice recordings, or precise geolocation about any child.
  • We do not allow children to use the app directly.
  • We do not share child-related data with third parties for profiling, advertising, or any purpose beyond what is strictly required to operate the app.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Personalize Activity Suggestions. Your child's age and preferences surface age-appropriate ideas from our curated catalog.
  • Improve the App. Aggregated and anonymized usage helps us develop new functionality.
  • Communicate With You. We use your email for transactional messages and, if you opt in, promotional content.
  • Provide Customer Support. If you contact us, we use your information to respond.

3. Children

Bunny Path is designed for parents and guardians. The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. The user of Bunny Path is an adult; child-related data is provided by that adult and used only to recommend age-appropriate activities.

  • Adult-only gate. Only adults (18+) may create accounts. The app contains no child-facing surface, in-app purchase prompts directed at children, or chat features.
  • Parent-supplied data only. Parents may optionally enter a child's first name or nickname and an age band. We do not collect last names, schools, photos, voice recordings, or precise geolocation about any child.
  • Minimum necessary. We store only what is required to surface age-appropriate activities inside that parent's own account.
  • Deletion at any time. Parents can delete child-related data at any time via Settings → Delete account, or by emailing privacy@bunnypath.com.
  • No personalized ads to children. The app is parent-facing and configures AdMob with non-personalized ads (npa=1), maximum content rating G, and tagForChildDirectedTreatment=false reflecting the parent-facing claim. We do not use child-related data for profiling, behavioral targeting, or cross-context advertising.
  • No sharing for marketing. We never sell, rent, or share child-related information with third parties for marketing.
  • COPPA contact. If you believe we have collected information from a child without parental consent, contact privacy@bunnypath.com; we will delete the information within 5 business days of verification.

4. Activity Catalog

Bunny Path's activity catalog is curated by our team, reviewed before publication, and published in periodic batches. Activities are filtered and ranked against this curated catalog using your preferences inside our own backend.

  • Curated Catalog. Activities are written and reviewed by our team before being added to the catalog. They are not generated in real time from your child's data.
  • No Cross-Site Profiling. We do not share child-related preferences with advertising or profiling networks.
  • Content Review. Activities pass internal review before publication. Always supervise your child during activities.

5. Advertising (AdMob)

Bunny Path may show advertisements supplied by Google AdMob to logged-in adult users. We configure AdMob in non-personalized mode (npa=1) with maximum content rating G. AdMob receives the device advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, GAID on Android, subject to your device-level permissions), IP address, and basic ad-event context (timestamp, app version, ad slot). AdMob does not receive your name, email, child name, or child age band.

  • Non-personalized only. Ads are contextual, not behavioral. AdMob does not build cross-app interest profiles about you from Bunny Path traffic.
  • No ads to children. The app is parent-facing and the user is an adult; see Children.
  • Opt-out. You can limit ad tracking on your device: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking (and → Apple Advertising); Android Settings → Privacy → Ads → Delete advertising ID. California residents may also exercise the Do Not Sell or Share right described below.

7. Data Storage and Security

  • Data is stored on a secure cloud database platform (Supabase, see Sub-processors).
  • All data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.2+).
  • Access is restricted to authorized personnel on a need-to-know basis with row-level security.
  • Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text.
  • In the event of a breach, we will notify affected users and relevant authorities per applicable law. Researchers can report vulnerabilities under our disclosure policy.

10. California residents: your privacy rights

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA gives you the rights described below. To exercise any of them, email privacy@bunnypath.com or use in-app Settings → Privacy. We respond within 45 days, extendable to 90 days where the request is complex (we will tell you if we need the extension).

Your rights

  • Right to know. The right to know what personal information we collect, use, disclose, and share, and how we use it.
  • Right to delete. The right to request deletion of personal information we have collected. This is also exposed in-app at Settings → Delete account; see also how long we keep your data.
  • Right to correct. The right to request correction of inaccurate personal information.
  • Right to limit use of sensitive personal information. Bunny Path does not use sensitive personal information for any purpose beyond what is necessary to operate the service, but you may still ask us to confirm this.
  • Right to opt out of sale or sharing. See below.

Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information

Bunny Path does not sell personal information for money. We share device identifiers with Google AdMob solely for non-personalized advertising (npa=1; see Advertising); under California law this may be considered "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising. To opt out:

  • On iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking (turn off "Allow Apps to Request to Track"), and Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising (turn off "Personalized Ads").
  • On Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads → "Delete advertising ID" (or toggle "Opt out of Ads Personalization").
  • Or email privacy@bunnypath.com with the subject line "Do Not Sell or Share" and we will record the request against your account.

Notice at Collection

At or before the point of collection, the categories of personal information we collect and the purposes are:

  • Identifiers (name, email, account ID, device identifiers including advertising ID), to operate your account, authenticate you, send transactional and (with consent) promotional messages, and serve non-personalized ads.
  • Customer records (parent-supplied child first name and age band, activity preferences), to surface age-appropriate activities inside your account.
  • Internet/network activity (features used, activity views, session duration, crash diagnostics), to operate, debug, and improve the app.
  • Commercial information (subscription status, receipt metadata), to fulfill purchases and keep tax records.
  • Geolocation, coarse, derived from IP only; we do not collect precise geolocation.

We retain each category for the period described in How long we keep your data.

Authorized agents

You may designate an authorized agent to exercise these rights on your behalf. We will ask the agent to provide written authorization signed by you and may ask you to verify your identity directly with us before completing the request.

No discrimination

We will not deny service, charge different prices, or provide a different level of service because you exercised any of these rights.

11. Users in the European Economic Area / UK

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and equivalent UK law give you the rights described below.

Legal basis for processing

  • Legitimate interest, operating and improving the app, securing accounts, preventing abuse, and serving non-personalized advertising.
  • Performance of a contract, providing the service you signed up for, fulfilling subscriptions, and customer support.
  • Consent, analytics and advertising where required, and marketing email; you may withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.
  • Legal obligation. Tax records on receipts and subscriptions, and responses to lawful requests from authorities.

Your rights

  • Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, and withdrawal of consent.
  • Right to lodge a complaint with your local Data Protection Authority. You can find your DPA via the European Data Protection Board's directory.

How to exercise your rights

Email privacy@bunnypath.com; we respond within 30 days. We may ask you to verify your identity before completing the request.

EU / UK contact and representation

We will appoint a formal Article 27 EU representative once user volume warrants it. Until then, the contact for EU and UK privacy matters is privacy@bunnypath.com.

International transfers

Most of our sub-processors (Supabase, Sentry, AdMob, Qonversion, OpenRouter, Apple, Google) operate in or transfer data to the United States. Where personal data is transferred out of the EEA / UK / Switzerland, we rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and the UK International Data Transfer Addendum, plus supplementary measures such as encryption in transit and at rest. The full sub-processor list is in Sub-processors.

14. Security & vulnerability disclosures

We take security seriously. If you believe you have found a vulnerability in Bunny Path, we want to hear from you.

How to report

Email security@bunnypath.com. Please include enough detail for us to reproduce the issue (URL or endpoint, steps, expected vs. observed behavior, and any proof-of-concept). PGP key available on request.

What we promise

  • We will acknowledge receipt within 72 hours.
  • We will not pursue legal action against good-faith researchers who follow this policy.
  • We will credit researchers in our disclosure notes (with permission) once a fix has shipped.

In scope

  • Authentication, session management, and account-takeover paths.
  • Data storage, anything that could expose, modify, or delete other users' data.
  • Payment integrity (subscription state, receipt validation).
  • Ad-network integrity (mis-attribution, click fraud against our account).
  • Server-side configuration of bunnypath.com, the marketing site, and the Cloudflare worker.

Out of scope

  • Social engineering of our staff, users, or vendors.
  • Physical attacks on our offices or hardware.
  • Denial-of-service or volumetric attacks.
  • Anything that requires the attacker to root or jailbreak the user's own device, or to have already compromised it.
  • Reports generated solely by automated scanners with no demonstrated impact.

Bounty

We do not currently run a paid bounty program. We offer credit and a sincere thank-you. We will revisit this as Bunny Path scales.
