Getting started

What is Bunny Path?

Bunny Path is a parenting app with 20,000+ hand-curated, off-screen play ideas for kids ages 0–12. You tell us your child's age and a few interests, and we surface activities you can do at home today, with stuff you already have.

It's designed for parents. Kids don't use the app directly. You pick something and play together (or hand them the prompt and supervise).

What ages is Bunny Path for?

Bunny Path covers ages 0 through 12, split across nine age bands so each activity is matched to a developmental stage:

  • Under 1 (0–12 mo) · Babies
  • 1–2 · Toddler
  • 2–3 · Explorer
  • 3–4 · Builder
  • 4–5 · Creator
  • 5–6 · Thinker
  • 7–8 · Maker
  • 9–10 · Adventurer
  • 11–12 · Strategist

You can change your child's age band any time in Settings → Profile, and the activity feed will re-tune.

Do I need to create an account?

Yes, a free account is required so your saved activities, child profiles, and subscription state can sync across devices and survive a re-install.

You can sign up with Apple, Google, or email. We never ask for a child's last name, school, address, or photo.

How does Bunny Path pick activities for me?

Each reveal shows you 3 fresh activities tailored to your child's age and the focus areas you chose during onboarding (creative, active, discovery, calm, etc.). Tap Load More for another batch of 3.

Free accounts get 15 activities per day (5 reveals × 3 activities). Premium removes the cap entirely — load as many fresh sets as you want.

Premium also unlocks deeper discovery. Tap any tag, material, or benefit on an activity card to find more activities like it. For example, tap the “fine motor” benefit and you’ll see every activity that builds the same skill. You can also search by activity type, by keywords in the description, by materials you already have at home, or by the developmental benefit you want to work on.

Subscription & billing

How much does Bunny Path cost?

Bunny Path is free to download with a generous free tier. Bunny Path Premium unlocks the full 20,000+ activity library and removes daily limits.

Premium pricing is shown in the in-app paywall in your local currency at the time of purchase. The price you see in the app at checkout is the price you'll be charged, taxes (where applicable) are added by Apple or Google.

Is there a free trial?

Yes, new subscribers get a 7-day free trial of Bunny Path Premium. You won't be charged until day 7, and you can cancel any time before then with no charge.

The trial is offered once per Apple ID / Google account. Restoring purchases won't restart it.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google, not by Bunny Path directly. To cancel:

iPhone / iPad:

  1. Open Settings on your device.
  2. Tap your name at the top, then tap Subscriptions.
  3. Tap Bunny Path, then Cancel Subscription.

Android:

  1. Open the Google Play Store app.
  2. Tap your profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
  3. Tap Bunny Path, then Cancel subscription.

You'll keep Premium access through the end of the period you've already paid for. Cancelling stops the next renewal. It doesn't issue an immediate refund.

I was charged after cancelling, what happened?

Subscriptions auto-renew until cancellation is confirmed by Apple or Google before the renewal date. The most common cause of a surprise charge is that the cancellation was started but not completed, or it was completed after the renewal had already processed.

Bunny Path doesn't process the payment, so we can't issue the refund directly. You'll need to request it from the store:

  • Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, find the Bunny Path charge, choose Request a refund.
  • Google: Open Play Store → menu → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & order history → tap the charge → Report an issue.
How do I restore purchases on a new device?

Sign in to Bunny Path on the new device using the same Apple ID or Google account you used to subscribe, then open Settings → Subscription → Restore purchases. Your Premium status will sync within a few seconds.

Subscriptions are tied to the store account that paid, not to your Bunny Path login. Make sure you're signed into the right Apple ID / Play account on the device.

Activities & content

Why don't you have activities for older kids (13+)?

Bunny Path is purpose-built for ages 0–12, that's where curated, parent-friendly play prompts have the highest impact and where our content review process is tuned to. Activities for teenagers need a very different content model (autonomy, friend dynamics, screen-balance) that we don't want to fake.

If you want ideas for an older sibling, the 11–12 band stretches reasonably well, and many activities scale up with materials and complexity.

Are the activities safe?

Every activity is written and reviewed by our team before it's added to the catalog. Each one is tagged with an age band and screened against safety rules, including a hard filter for choking, sharp-tool, burn, and toxic-substance hazards relative to the child's age. Materials shown on each activity card are checked for age appropriateness.

That said, no rule replaces a parent in the room. Always supervise your child during play, use your judgment about your specific child's abilities, and skip anything that doesn't feel right. If you spot an activity you think is unsafe for the listed age, tap the report icon on the activity card — we re-review every report.

Can I save my favorites?

Saving favorites is a Premium feature. Tap the heart icon on any activity to save it — your saved list lives in Profile → Saved and syncs across devices when you're signed in.

Premium accounts get unlimited saves. Free accounts don't have access to Saved; upgrade anytime to start building your library.

I disagreed with an activity. How do I report it?

Open the activity, tap the ••• menu in the top-right, and choose Report. Tell us what felt off, wrong age, unsafe materials, unclear steps, anything. The report includes the activity ID automatically so we can find it instantly, and we re-review every report.

Privacy & data

What data does Bunny Path collect?

The minimum we need to run the app: your account email, the optional first name / age band you enter for each child, your activity preferences, and standard usage and crash data. We don't ask for last names, school names, addresses, photos, voice recordings, or precise location.

Full details are in our Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings → Account → Delete account. Confirm, and your account and all child profiles are deleted from our active systems immediately. Encrypted backups are purged within 30 days after that.

Account deletion is permanent and unrecoverable. We can't restore profiles, saved activities, or subscription state once it's done. Cancel any active subscription through the App Store or Play Store first if you don't want it to renew.

Do you share data with advertisers?

We never share child-related data with anyone for advertising or profiling.

The free tier shows non-personalized banner ads through Google AdMob. These ads are configured with the npa=1 flag, meaning they're contextual only, based on the app, not on you or your child. Premium subscribers see no ads at all.

For the full breakdown, see the Privacy Policy.

Technical issues

The app crashes on launch. What do I do?

Try these in order:

  1. Force-quit and reopen. Swipe Bunny Path out of the app switcher, then tap the icon again.
  2. Restart the device. Clears any stuck background state.
  3. Update the app. Check the App Store / Play Store for a newer build.
  4. Reinstall. Delete the app and download it again. Your account, saves, and subscription will restore once you sign back in.

If it still crashes after a reinstall, the App Store or Play Store may have a newer build with the fix. Otherwise, leave us a review noting your device model (e.g. iPhone 14, Pixel 8) and OS version (e.g. iOS 17.4, Android 14) so we can spot the cause.

I'm not getting daily reminders.

Most reminder issues are a notification-permission setting. Check, in this order:

  • Inside Bunny Path: Settings → Notifications. Make sure the daily reminder is on and set to a time you're awake for.
  • iOS: Phone Settings → Notifications → Bunny Path, allow notifications, banners, and sounds.
  • Android: Phone Settings → Apps → Bunny Path → Notifications. Allow notifications and disable battery-saver / Doze restrictions for the app.
  • Focus / Do Not Disturb: If the reminder time falls inside a Focus mode, the notification can get silently filtered. Add Bunny Path to the allowed apps for that mode.
Activities aren't loading.

Activities are fetched from our servers, so a connection issue is the usual culprit:

  1. Check that your device has internet (try opening a website in your browser).
  2. Pull-to-refresh on the home or library screen.
  3. Force-quit the app and reopen it.
  4. Sign out (Settings → Account → Sign out) and sign back in.

If activities are still blank after that, try a different network (switch from Wi-Fi to cellular, or vice versa). Some restrictive networks (school, corporate, hotel Wi-Fi) block the API endpoints the app needs.
