What's new in Bunny Path

A quick log of the changes you'll notice in each TestFlight update.

We ship small, frequent updates focused on the moments parents actually use the app, picking a quick activity, marking it done, sharing a favorite. Here's what landed in the most recent builds.

Recent builds

  1. Build 94: stability & safety polish
  2. Build 93: saved screen & share fixes
  3. Build 92: faster Home, smoother search
  4. Build 91: onboarding & profile refinements
  5. Build 90: tab bar polish & new pedagogies
  6. Build 89: bunny tab bar fixes
  7. Build 88: search hotfix
  8. Build 87: profile & explore
  9. Build 86: age-aware collections
Build 94 May 2026

Stability & safety polish

  • Tightened our review pass on activity descriptions so age guidance and material lists read clearly.
  • Smoothed out a handful of layout glitches reported on smaller iPhones.
  • Background performance work to keep the app snappy as the catalog keeps growing.
Build 93 May 2026

Saved screen & share fixes

  • Saved activities now stay sorted the way you left them after coming back to the app.
  • Sharing an activity to Messages or email keeps formatting clean and includes a preview image.
  • Fixed a small flash when opening an activity card from the Home screen.
Build 92 May 2026

Faster Home, smoother search

  • Home screen loads noticeably faster on a cold launch.
  • Search results update as you type with less lag, especially when filtering by interest.
  • Resolved a rare crash when switching profiles mid-scroll.
Build 91 May 2026

Onboarding & profile refinements

  • The first-time setup flow is shorter and clearer about what each step does.
  • Editing a child's age or interests now updates suggestions immediately. No need to restart the app.
  • Small wording cleanups across Settings so labels match what each toggle actually does.
Build 90 May 2026

Tab bar polish & more learning styles

  • Refreshed the bottom tab bar, clearer color contrast and a smoother active-tab indicator.
  • The plus button in the tab bar now pops out cleanly and is easier to hit one-handed.
  • Added five new learning-style filters so you can match activities to how your child actually plays.
  • Quality-of-life cleanups across the activity card and saved screen.
Build 89 May 2026

Bunny tab bar fixes

  • Fixed the green ring around the bunny tab so the active state reads correctly in both light and dark mode.
  • Resolved a small inconsistency when carrying tags between screens.
Build 88 April 2026

Search hotfix

  • Restored fast tag search results that had regressed in build 87, searching by interest now feels instant again.
Build 87 April 2026

Profile activities & faster Explore

  • Fixed a bug where a child's recently completed activities weren't showing up on their profile.
  • Polished the bottom tab bar (round one).
  • Sped up the Explore tab so similar activities load noticeably faster, especially on older iPhones.
Build 86 April 2026

Age-aware collections + 50+ fixes

  • Collections (themes, weekend ideas, seasonal lists) now adapt to each child's age band so you stop seeing toddler crafts when your kid is seven.
  • More than 50 small bug fixes across navigation, sharing, and the activity detail screen.
  • Home-screen rings honor your local timezone correctly and share buttons work everywhere they should.
